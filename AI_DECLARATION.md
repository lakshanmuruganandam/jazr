# AI-use declaration

We used AI tools to support this work, as the ATRC ATP 2026 rules allow:

- **Claude (Anthropic):** research and literature search, engineering calculations, simulation and test code, the dashboard and the 3D digital-twin code, drafting text, and reviewing the design for flaws.
- **Gemini (Google, via Antigravity):** concept visualisation images (photoreal renders of the proposed system).
- **Microsoft neural text-to-speech:** the voice-over in our films.

The team reviewed every design decision, assumption and result. Every number traces to our own simulations, test files or cited public sources. Renders are illustrations, not photographs of a built system. No physical prototype has been tested yet; the tank test is our next step. Every team member can explain the work in their own words.
