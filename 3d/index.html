About / Notes

Illustrative layout · AI-free procedural 3D · not the real plant. JAZR v5 (moving belt screen, Layer 2 hydro-jet curtain, HydroLift water-only float chambers, RELEASE alive near/far). Generic buildings, no real plant drawings. Blue Blubber jellyfish (Catostylus sp.; Gulf records mostly C. perezi) drawn ≈3× life size; outlets drawn closer than real; motion time-compressed. Model-based; no physical prototype yet.

Counters are a simple illustrative model (0.33 kg per animal, measured mean). They are labelled model. BEFORE means today, no JAZR (same bloom, same period). WITH JAZR means with JAZR v5. TOTAL SAVED ALIVE counts every animal caught and kept alive: collector + HydroLift + pipe + released. The plant clock defaults to ×600. The 2.9 h, 10.5 km pipe transit is shown ×20 faster than the plant clock (adjustable in Parameters), so releases at the outlet appear within seconds. The physics is unchanged: ≈1 m/s in a 1.6 m pipe, O₂ held, no bubbles. The deploy animation is time-compressed; the modelled deploy time is ≈10 min (573 s).

Visual-only, not physics: bells are drawn ≈3× life size (larger in split view). Drawn leakers are exaggerated so that Layer 2 can be seen working. Outlets are drawn closer than real. Motion is time-compressed.

Sources: JAZR_v5_FINAL.md and JAZR/TESTS (model-based; no physical prototype). See the engineering folder in the GitHub repository.
